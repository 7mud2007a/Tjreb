I — The Essence

Not made to follow the room. Made to change it.

ROAND is composed for a single purpose: to alter the air around you. Every accord is engineered not to blend in, but to announce itself — quietly, then completely. This is not a fragrance worn in the background.

II — The Composition

Three movements. One signature.

  • Top

    Bergamot · Pink Pepper · Saffron

    The opening — bright, unexpected, gone too soon.

  • Heart

    Oud · Iris · Leather

    The center — where the character reveals itself.

  • Base

    Amber · Sandalwood · Musk

    The signature — what remains on skin, and in memory.

III — The Object

A vessel, not a bottle.

  • Hand-finished glass, weighted for the palm.
  • A cap cut like stone, cast in aged bronze.
  • The amber, held in light.
  • Numbered. Never repeated.

IV — The Ritual

One spray. A room rewritten.

ROAND does not fade politely into the day. It reorganizes it — the air after, unmistakably different from the air before.

V — The Maison

Made in scarcity, by design.

ROAND is not produced to meet demand. It is produced to exceed it — and then stop.

  1. 01 Rarity Each batch is limited, numbered, and never reissued.
  2. 02 Craft Blended by hand. Aged in darkness before it ever meets light.
  3. 03 Permanence Built to be remembered, not repeated.
